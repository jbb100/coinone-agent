### 1. Bitcoin Halving Cycle Overview

**The 4-Year Pattern**:
Bitcoin undergoes a "halving" approximately every 4 years, where the block reward for miners is cut in half. Historically, this has created a repeating market cycle.

**Halving History**:
| Halving | Date | Block Reward | Price at Halving | Cycle Peak | Peak Price |
|---------|------|--------------|------------------|------------|------------|
| 1st | Nov 2012 | 50 → 25 BTC | ~$12 | Dec 2013 | ~$1,100 |
| 2nd | Jul 2016 | 25 → 12.5 BTC | ~$650 | Dec 2017 | ~$20,000 |
| 3rd | May 2020 | 12.5 → 6.25 BTC | ~$8,500 | Nov 2021 | ~$69,000 |
| 4th | Apr 2024 | 6.25 → 3.125 BTC | ~$64,000 | TBD | TBD |
| 5th | Est. 2028 | 3.125 → 1.5625 BTC | TBD | TBD | TBD |

### 2. Cycle Phases

**Phase 1: Accumulation (6-12 months post-bottom)**
- Price stabilization after major decline
- Low volatility, low interest
- Smart money accumulating
- Sentiment: Disbelief, Depression
- Strategy: Heavy accumulation, DCA

**Phase 2: Early Bull (6-12 months)**
- Price breaks above key MAs
- Increasing volume
- Early adopters entering
- Sentiment: Hope, Optimism
- Strategy: Continue accumulation, reduce DCA frequency

**Phase 3: Parabolic Bull (3-6 months)**
- Explosive price increases
- Mainstream media attention
- Retail FOMO
- Sentiment: Belief, Thrill, Euphoria
- Strategy: Take profits, set trailing stops

**Phase 4: Distribution/Top (1-3 months)**
- Price volatility at maximum
- "New paradigm" narratives
- Smart money exiting
- Sentiment: Euphoria, Complacency
- Strategy: Aggressive profit-taking

**Phase 5: Bear Market (12-18 months)**
- Sustained price decline
- 70-85% drawdown typical
- Capitulation events
- Sentiment: Anxiety, Denial, Panic, Capitulation
- Strategy: Preserve capital, prepare for accumulation

**Sentiment Metrics**:
- Fear & Greed Index extremes
- Google Trends for "Bitcoin"
- Social media activity levels

### 4. Is the 4-Year Cycle Still Valid?

**Arguments FOR Cycle Continuation**:
- Supply shock from halving is real and ongoing
- Previous cycles show consistent patterns
- Adoption curve follows predictable path

**Arguments AGAINST (Cycle Evolution)**:
- Institutional adoption smoothing volatility
- ETF flows changing market dynamics
- Market maturation reducing extremes
- Possible supercycle theory
- Macro factors (rates, liquidity) having more impact

**Current Consensus (2026)**:
The cycle may be elongating or dampening in amplitude, but the halving supply shock remains a fundamental driver. However, traditional "blow-off top" patterns may be replaced by more gradual price appreciation.

**Portfolio Allocation by Phase**:
